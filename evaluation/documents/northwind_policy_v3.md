# Northwind Technologies Employee Policy

This policy sets out the working arrangements, leave entitlements and benefits for all full-time employees of Northwind Technologies Private Limited. It should be read together with your employment contract.

## Working Hours and Location

Northwind Technologies is headquartered in Pune, and the Pune head office is the primary place of work for all teams. Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to prior manager approval. Remote days must be agreed with the line manager in advance, and are not carried over from one week to the next.

## Leave Entitlements

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests are submitted through the HR portal and must be approved by the line manager. In addition, employees receive 12 public holidays each year, which HR publishes at the start of the year. The company provides 12 weeks of paid parental leave. This applies to mothers and fathers alike.

## Employment Terms

All new employees serve a probation period of 6 months, during which performance is reviewed every month with the line manager. After probation, either the employee or the company must give 45 days of written notice to end the employment. Notice periods cannot be waived without written agreement from HR.

## Compensation and Benefits

Salary is paid on the last working day of each month by bank transfer. The company provides group health insurance for every employee with a sum insured of INR 5 lakh, renewed each year on the policy anniversary. Employees can add eligible family members to the cover through the HR portal.

## Travel and Expenses

Managers may approve business travel expenses of up to INR 40,000 per trip without further sign-off. Anything above this limit requires approval from the department head before booking. Claims must be submitted with receipts within the following month.
